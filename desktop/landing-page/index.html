Beta notes

Honest about where detection stands.

  • Ships with a stock, general-purpose detector, not yet fine-tuned on real Sentry footage
  • It over-flags. Of the beta feedback so far, most clips Mimir rated Important were rated lower by the person who looked at them. Treat Important as "look at this", not "something happened" - and telling us when it's wrong is the single most useful thing you can do, because the detector can't improve without examples.
  • Two rules were tightened on 5 August after reading that feedback back, both cases of motion near the car being mistaken for something touching it. Replayed against the feedback, it removes 17 of the 18 Important calls a human rejected and keeps the one they agreed with - dropping them to Review, so nothing is hidden. That is 19 clips written in by people who thought something looked wrong, so it's a hint, not an accuracy claim. It should over-flag less than it did. It still over-flags.
  • Rear-end contact and door-ding detection are the current priority to improve
  • Report what it gets wrong: feedback.mimir@gmail.com

Before you download

What it needs to run.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; ~197 MB download, ~325 MB installed
  • Scanning and review are fully offline - your footage never leaves the PC on its own
  • Internet is used only if you choose to send feedback or a contribution, and for install if Windows is missing the WebView2 runtime (Windows 11 already includes it)
  • No account, and no Ollama unless you want the optional local AI second opinion
  • A DirectX 12 GPU is not required, but it matters: object detection is over half the scan time and runs roughly 10x slower on CPU. With a GPU expect around 25 minutes for a full 679-clip TeslaCam dump; without one, budget hours. The app's system check tells you which you have before you start.
  • The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box. Click More info, then Run anyway. That warning is accurate - it means the publisher is unverified, not that the file is safe. Certificates cost money and this is free.

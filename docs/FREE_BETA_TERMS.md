# Mimir Free Private Beta Terms

Last updated: 2026-07-22

Mimir is currently a free, invite-only Windows beta. There are no subscriptions,
payments, activation servers, or automatic charges. Access is controlled only by
who receives the installer.

Mimir is a review assistant, not a definitive accident, crime, insurance, or legal
decision system. It can miss important footage and can flag ordinary activity.
The user remains responsible for reviewing source video and deciding what happened.

The beta is provided as-is for evaluation. Back up important footage before using
file-moving features. Scanning itself must not modify source clips. A beta build has
no destructive expiry mechanism and does not require an account or internet access
after installation.

Beta users may submit diagnostics or footage only through a separate, explicit
action. Participation does not grant Mimir permission to collect footage in the
background. Any future paid product will use separate terms and a separate decision.

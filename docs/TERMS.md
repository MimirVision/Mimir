# Mimir beta terms

Last updated 12 August 2026. These cover the free public beta.

Mimir is not a company yet. It is one person's software, given away, and these
terms say so plainly rather than borrowing language from a business that does
not exist.

---

## 1. What you are getting

Mimir scans video on your own computer and sorts it. It is early beta software.
It is free, and there is no warranty of any kind — if it misses something, gets
something wrong, or stops working, you have no claim against the author beyond
what the law gives you regardless.

**It over-flags.** On a real week of footage it puts roughly four events in ten
in front of you. Treat *Important* as "look at this", never as "something
happened". See the
[known issues](../desktop/release_assets/KNOWN_ISSUES.md) for the current
numbers.

Mimir does not prove that anything touched your car, does not identify people,
and does not read number plates. Nothing it produces is a legal, insurance, or
safety determination.

## 2. Your footage stays yours

Scanning happens entirely on your machine. There is no account, no cloud, no
telemetry, no analytics. Nothing about your footage leaves your computer as a
side effect of using Mimir.

Two things can leave, and only when you press a button:

- **Feedback** — your verdict on an incident, and optionally the clip it refers to.
- **A contribution** — footage you deliberately send to improve the detector.

Both are encrypted on your machine before they are sent.

## 3. What you promise about anything you send

This is the important part, and it applies to feedback clips as much as to
contributions.

**You must have the right to every clip you upload.** By sending one you
confirm that:

- you recorded it yourself, or you have permission from whoever did; and
- sending it to Mimir does not break any law, contract, or other person's
  rights; and
- you are not sending footage obtained unlawfully.

Dashcam footage routinely contains other people who never agreed to anything.
In most of Europe that makes you the controller of that footage, and the
decision to share it is yours to make and yours to stand behind. If you are not
sure you have the right to send a clip, do not send it.

**Do not send** footage you found online, footage from someone else's vehicle
without their permission, or anything recorded somewhere you were not allowed
to record.

### Why there is still a tick-box per contribution

Because this section is a promise, and the receipt is a record.

If someone later asks for their car to be removed from the training set, a
promise you made when you downloaded the software does not say which upload was
yours, when, or on what basis. The receipt does. It exists so a request like
that can be answered in minutes rather than not at all.

It asks for your name and how you came by the footage. If you own it, there is
nothing else to fill in.

## 4. What is done with a contribution

Contributed footage is used to measure and improve Mimir's detection. It is
not published, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

You can ask for a contribution to be deleted. Quote the package id shown when
you sent it, or the approximate date, to <feedback.mimir@gmail.com>. Anything
already used to train a released model cannot be pulled back out of that model,
which is a limitation of how training works rather than a policy.

## 5. What you may not do

- Use Mimir to identify people, track individuals, or monitor somewhere you
  have no right to monitor.
- Present its output as proof of fault, or as an insurance or legal finding.
- Send footage you do not have the right to send.

## 6. Ending

You can stop using Mimir at any time by uninstalling it; nothing is left
running. The author may stop distributing it at any time. Neither ends the
promises in section 3 about footage already sent.

## 7. Law

Norwegian law. Mandatory consumer rights are not affected by anything above.

## 8. Changes

These terms will change — a company is being formed, and a paid version is
planned. Material changes will be announced with the release that carries them.
**A paid product will use separate terms and a separate decision**; nothing here
signs you up to anything future.

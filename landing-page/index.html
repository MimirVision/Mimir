How it works

From folder to review timeline.

  • Choose footage folder
  • Scan locally
  • Review incidents
  • Move or trash clips manually

Safety

You stay in control.

  • No automatic deletion
  • Manual status control
  • Local processing so you stay in control of your privacy

Beta notes

Honest about where detection stands.

  • Ships with a stock, general-purpose detector, not yet fine-tuned on real Sentry footage
  • Can miss real events and can flag ordinary activity - you're always the final review
  • Rear-end contact and door-ding detection are the current priority to improve
  • Report what it gets wrong: feedback.mimir@gmail.com

Before you download

What it needs to run.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; ~395 MB download, ~325 MB installed
  • No internet needed to install or scan - everything is bundled
  • No GPU, no Ollama, no account required for the core app
  • Unsigned installer: Windows will show a warning, choose the advanced option to continue
